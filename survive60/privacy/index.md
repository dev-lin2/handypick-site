# Survive 60 Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game **Survive 60** (the "game") uses, why, and the
choices you have. The game is made by **Handypick** ("we", "us"), the developer named on the
game's Google Play page.

Questions or requests: **support@handypick.dev**

## The short version

- Survive 60 is a single-player game. There is no chat and no friends list, and we do not create an account
  for you. You can **optionally sign in with Google Play Games** for achievements, leaderboards and cloud save.
- **We do not run any servers** and we do not sell any personal information about you. Your progress is
  saved on your device, and, only if you sign in to Google Play Games, also in **your own Play Games account**.
- The game uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to see how the game is played (section 9). It never sends
  your name or anything you type. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.
- The game shows **ads from Google AdMob**. Google's ad software collects some device data (described
  below) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and
  Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first.
- **Purchases are handled by Google Play.** We never see your payment details.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your phone or tablet, in the game's private app storage:

- Your game progress: heroes, items, Gems, Ascension Points, settings, quests and statistics.
- Which in-app purchases you own, so the game can give you what you bought.
- Sound, display and privacy settings (including your choice for "Share anonymous play data").
- A random value created on install, used to encrypt and sign your save files so they cannot be edited
  by accident or by other apps.
- If you sign in to Google Play Games: your Play Games player ID and the time of your last cloud save, so
  the game knows which achievements it has already sent to your account.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us. The only exceptions are the optional cloud save
(section 10) and the anonymous play data described in section 9.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows **rewarded video ads** that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. It never
shows ads between games or during a game.
Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the
EEA and Switzerland).

When ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the game **collects and shares with Google**:

| Data | Examples | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IP address | Your device's internet address, which may be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not precise location) | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Interactions with ads and the game | App launches, taps, ad views | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Diagnostic information | App launch time, freezes, energy use | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Device identifiers | Android Advertising ID, App Set ID | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |

This list follows Google's own published disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK:
<https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure>.
Data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive this data ourselves; Google processes it under its own
privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. How Google uses data from apps that show its ads:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

**Your choices about ads**

- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland).** Before any ad is loaded, the game shows Google's consent form. You
  can accept or refuse personalized ads. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalized or
  limited ads, which use less data. You can change your choice at any time from
  **Settings > Privacy options** in the game.
- **Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device's
  settings (usually *Settings > Privacy > Ads* or *Settings > Google > Ads*). After deletion, apps
  cannot use it for ads.
- **US state privacy laws.** You can contact us at the address above with any request. Because we hold
  no ad data about you, we will point you to Google's tools where needed.
- **Remove Ads.** The paid Remove Ads item gives you rewarded-ad bonuses at once, without a video. The
  game still starts Google's ad software, because the same consent step is used for analytics.

## 3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy in-game items (Gems, perks, boosts, Remove Ads, bundles) through **Google Play Billing**.
Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. **We never see your name, email
address, card or other payment details.** The game only receives from Google Play a confirmation of what
you bought (the product and a purchase reference), checks it on your device and gives you the item. We do
not run a server. The items you own are kept in your saved progress on your device, and in your Play Games
cloud save if you use it. If analytics is on, the game also reports which product was bought and its list
price (never payment details, section 9).

- Permanent items (perks, boosts, Remove Ads, bundles) can be **restored** from Google Play on a new
  device or after reinstalling, through the game's Store.
- **Gems are a consumable.** Once bought they are added to your saved progress. If you uninstall the game
  or clear its data, they are lost, unless your progress is in your Play Games cloud save (section 10).

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or
  microphone or camera data. The game does not ask for those permissions.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not use crash reporting or advertising trackers of our own. We use Google Analytics for Firebase
  only as described in section 9.
- The game has no chat, no friends list and no content made by other players. We do not create user accounts.

## 5. Children

Survive 60 is **not directed at children**. It is intended for players aged **16** and older, and it
contains in-app purchases and chests with random rewards. We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children, and we run no servers where such information could be stored. If you believe a child has
used the game and want advice on limiting ads or analytics data, contact us at the address above.

## 6. How long information is kept and how to delete it

- **On your device:** your saves and settings stay until you delete them. To delete everything, uninstall
  the game or use *Settings > Apps > Survive 60 > Storage > Clear storage* on your device. This also
  deletes your progress and any Gems that are not in a cloud save; it cannot be undone.
- **In your Play Games account (cloud save):** the cloud save stays in your account until you delete it.
  You can delete this game's data, or your whole Play Games profile, in the settings of the Google Play
  Games app or at <https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy>.
- **With Google:** data collected by Google's ad software is kept and deleted according to Google's
  policies (<https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention>). You can reset or delete your
  Advertising ID as described in section 2. Analytics data is kept for **14 months**, then deleted by
  Google.
- **With us:** we hold no data about you on servers of our own. We can see analytics only as totals and
  reports in Google's tools, linked to a random app instance ID, not to you. If you email us, we keep the
  email only as long as needed to answer it.

## 7. Security

Your save files are encrypted and signed on your device. The Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google Analytics for
Firebase, Google Play Games and Google Play Billing send their data using encrypted connections (TLS). No
method is perfectly secure, but we do not store your information on servers of our own.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or US state privacy
laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data.
Because we do not collect or hold personal data on servers of our own, most requests are best made
directly to Google for ad, analytics and Play Games data (<https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy>).
For analytics data, you can also switch analytics off in the game. You may still contact us at
**support@handypick.dev** and we will help.

Google may process ad, analytics and Play Games data on servers in other countries, including the United
States, under its own safeguards.

## 9. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played (for example how many players finish their first game, which
battles are too hard, or where players stop) we use **Google Analytics for Firebase**, a service of
Google that processes the data for us. It collects:

- A random app instance ID created for this install of the game. It is not your name, not your
  Google account and **not** your Android Advertising ID (we have switched off its collection in
  Firebase).
- Game events, such as starting or finishing a game, reaching a day, a battle's result, opening a
  menu, opening a chest, upgrading a perk or a building, watching a rewarded ad, opening the Store and
  the result of a purchase (the product and its list price, never payment details).
- A few summary values about your progress, such as your hero classes, your highest Trial, a rough count
  of games played, whether you bought something or own Remove Ads, and whether you are signed in to
  Google Play Games (yes or no only).
- Device and app information that the Firebase software adds by itself: device model, Android
  version, language, app version, and your country and region estimated from your IP address.

The game never sends your name, your email, your Google Play Games name or player ID, anything you type,
or your save file. We use the data only in aggregate to improve the game, never for advertising, and we do
not use it to identify you. Ad personalization from this data and Google signals (linking with Google
accounts) are switched off. Test versions of the game send nothing. Google processes the data under
<https://policies.google.com/privacy> and <https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy> and keeps it for
**14 months**.

**Your choice:** in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics starts only after you answer the
consent form shown at the first launch, and only if you allow it. Everywhere, you can switch it off
at any time in the game: **Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous play data**. When it is off, the
game sends no analytics events. This switch is a setting of your device and is not part of your cloud save.

## 10. Google Play Games (optional)

The game can use **Google Play Games** for achievements, leaderboards and cloud save. Signing in is
optional: if you have a Play Games profile, Android may sign you in automatically when the game starts, and
you can also sign in from **Settings > Google Play Games**. Everything in the game works without signing in.

When you are signed in:

- Google shares your Play Games profile (player name and player ID) with the game. The game shows your
  name in Settings and keeps the player ID on your device (section 1). It never sends them to us or to
  analytics.
- Your achievements and your leaderboard scores (the deepest Abyss rank you passed and the longest day you
  reached in one game) are sent to your Play Games account. Leaderboards show your Play Games name and score
  to other players, as set in your Play Games profile.
- **Cloud save:** the game saves a copy of your whole progress (heroes, items, Gems, Ascension Points,
  purchases owned, quests and statistics) in your own Play Games account, using Google's Saved Games
  service. A short summary (highest Trial, Ascension Points earned, Gems, games finished) is stored with it
  so you can choose between saves. We cannot see or read your cloud save. You can load it on another device
  from **Settings > Google Play Games**.

Google Play Games is a Google service and works under <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. To stop using
it, sign out in the Google Play Games app. To delete the cloud save and your achievements for this game,
see section 6.
