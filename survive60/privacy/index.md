# Survive 60 Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game **Survive 60** (the "game") uses, why, and the
choices you have. The game is made by **Handypick** ("we", "us"), the developer named on the
game's Google Play page.

Questions or requests: **support@handypick.dev**

## The short version

- Survive 60 is a single-player game. There are **no accounts**, no logins, no chat and no friends list.
- **We do not run any servers** and we do not collect, receive or sell any personal information about you.
  Your progress is saved **only on your device**.
- The game shows **ads from Google AdMob**. Google's ad software collects some device data (described
  below) to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and
  Switzerland, the game asks for your consent first.
- **Purchases are handled by Google Play.** We never see your payment details.
- The game does **not** use analytics or tracking tools of its own.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your phone or tablet, in the game's private app storage:

- Your game progress: heroes, items, Gems, Ascension Points, settings, quests and statistics.
- Which in-app purchases you own, so the game can give you what you bought.
- Sound and display settings.
- A random value created on install, used to encrypt and sign your save files so they cannot be edited
  by accident or by other apps.

This information **never leaves your device** and is not sent to us or to anyone else. The game does not
back it up to the cloud.

The game also reads your device's **language and region setting** (for example "en_GB") on the device
itself, to decide whether paid chests may be offered where local law restricts them. This setting is not
sent anywhere.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows **rewarded video ads** that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus.
Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the
EEA and Switzerland).

When ads are enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the game **collects and shares with Google**:

| Data | Examples | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IP address | Your device's internet address, which may be used to estimate your general location (city or region, not precise location) | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Interactions with ads and the game | App launches, taps, ad views | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Diagnostic information | App launch time, freezes, energy use | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |
| Device identifiers | Android Advertising ID, App Set ID | Advertising, analytics, fraud prevention |

This list follows Google's own published disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK:
<https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure>.
Data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive this data ourselves; Google processes it under its own
privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. How Google uses data from apps that show its ads:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

**Your choices about ads**

- **Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland).** Before any ad is loaded, the game shows Google's consent form. You
  can accept or refuse personalized ads. If you refuse, Google may still show non-personalized or
  limited ads, which use less data. You can change your choice at any time from
  **Settings > Privacy options** in the game.
- **Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device's
  settings (usually *Settings > Privacy > Ads* or *Settings > Google > Ads*). After deletion, apps
  cannot use it for ads.
- **US state privacy laws.** You can contact
  us at the address above with any request; because we hold no data about you, we will point you to
  Google's tools where needed.
- **Remove Ads.** The paid Remove Ads item gives rewarded-ad bonuses at once, without
  a video (the game shows no ads between games). Google's ad software may still start
  in the game, as described above.

## 3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy in-game items (Gems, perks, boosts, Remove Ads, bundles) through **Google Play Billing**.
Google Play processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. **We never see your name, email
address, card or other payment details.** The game only receives from Google Play a confirmation of what
you bought (the product and a purchase reference), checks it on your device and gives you the item. We do
not run a server and do not store your purchase information anywhere except on your device.

- Permanent items (perks, boosts, Remove Ads, bundles) can be **restored** from Google Play on a new
  device or after reinstalling, through the game's Store.
- **Gems are a consumable.** Once bought they are added to your saved progress on that device. Like the
  rest of your progress, they **cannot be restored** if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location or
  microphone or camera data. The game does not ask for those permissions.
- We do not sell personal information.
- We do not use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools of our own.
- The game has no social features, chat or user accounts.

## 5. Children

Survive 60 is **not directed at children**. It is intended for players aged **16** and older, and it contains in-app purchases and chests with random
rewards. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we have no accounts or
servers where such information could be stored. If you believe a child has used the game and want
advice on limiting ads data, contact us at the address above.

## 6. How long information is kept and how to delete it

- **On your device:** your saves and settings stay until you delete them. To delete everything, uninstall
  the game or use *Settings > Apps > Survive 60 > Storage > Clear storage* on your device. This also
  deletes your progress and any Gems; it cannot be undone.
- **With Google:** data collected by Google's ad software is kept and deleted according to Google's
  policies (<https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention>). You can reset or delete your
  Advertising ID as described in section 2.
- **With us:** we hold nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you email us, we keep the
  email only as long as needed to answer it.

## 7. Security

Your save files are encrypted and signed on your device. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play
Billing send their data using encrypted connections (TLS). No method is perfectly secure, but the game
itself stores no information off your device.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or US state privacy
laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data.
Because we do not collect or hold personal data, most requests are best made directly to Google for
ad data (<https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy>). You may still contact us at
**support@handypick.dev** and we will help.

Google may process ad data on servers in other countries, including the United States, under its own
safeguards.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If the change is
significant (for example if we add analytics), we will also say so in the game update notes.
