# Survive 60 Pre-registration Reward Terms

**Last updated:** November 6, 2026

These terms cover the pre-registration reward for the Android game **Survive 60**, made by **Handypick**
("we", "us").

## The reward: Founder's Gift

- **5,000 Gems**, the game's in-game currency.
- The **Founder frame**, a cosmetic border on your hero portrait, kept forever.

## Who gets it

- Players who pre-register for Survive 60 on its Google Play page while pre-registration is open.
- The reward is linked to the Google account you used to pre-register. Google Play handles pre-registration and
  delivers the reward to that account when the game is released.

## How it is delivered

- Install Survive 60 from Google Play with the same Google account and open the game. The reward is added the first
  time the game starts and connects to Google Play; a "Thank you for pre-registering" card shows what you received.
- The Gems are given once per Google account. If you reinstall the game or play on another device with the same
  account, the Founder frame is restored, but the Gems are not given again.

## Other terms

- The reward has no cash value. It cannot be sold, exchanged for money, or transferred to another account.
- The reward is not for sale and is never offered as a purchase.
- If the game is not available in your country when it is released, the reward cannot be delivered there.
- We may correct a reward that was given by mistake (for example a duplicate caused by a technical error).
- Google Play's own terms also apply to pre-registration.

Privacy: see the [Survive 60 Privacy Policy](/survive60/privacy/).
